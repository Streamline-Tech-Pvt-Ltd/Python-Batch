ABOUT THE THAR

The Mahindra Thar is one of the most popular SUVs in India, designed for both city roads and off-road adventures. It features a bold and rugged design, powerful petrol and diesel engine options, and a 4x4 drivetrain for excellent performance on rough terrains such as mountains, deserts, and muddy roads. The Thar offers a comfortable cabin with modern features like a touchscreen infotainment system, Bluetooth connectivity, steering-mounted controls, automatic climate control, and advanced safety features including dual airbags, ABS with EBD, Electronic Stability Control (ESC), and hill-hold assist. Its strong build quality, high ground clearance, and reliable performance make it a favorite choice for adventure enthusiasts and families. The Mahindra Thar combines style, comfort, safety, and durability, making it one of the best lifestyle SUVs available in India.
